Money Tree AI User guide

A desktop bot that watches US stocks, reads the news, learns from its own results and trades through your own Alpaca account, inside the limits you set.

Read this first. Money Tree AI is software, not financial advice. Trading stocks can lose money, also more than you expect. Past results, including the bot's own tests, do not promise future results. Start with practice money. Only use money you can afford to lose. You are responsible for every trade the bot makes on your account.

1. Install

  1. Run MoneyTreeAI-Setup.exe. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 1 GB free space and 8 GB of memory are enough.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This shows for new programs that are not yet widely downloaded.
  3. Open Money Tree AI from the Start menu or the desktop. The logo shows at once; the window opens after 5 to 15 seconds.
  4. Only one copy runs at a time. If you click the icon again, the open window comes to the front.

2. First start

The app asks a few questions: your language, how long you hold trades, which market, and who decides on a buy. Each answer changes what the bot does. You can change all of them later in Modes and Settings.

It then asks how much of your computer the strategy bots may use. Full is fastest. Light keeps the computer cool.

3. Connect Alpaca

The bot trades only through Alpaca, a US broker with an API and no commission on US stocks. Many countries can open an account; check that Alpaca accepts yours.

  1. Make an Alpaca account. Its paper (practice) account is free and ready at once.
  2. In Alpaca, open API Keys and make a key pair. Copy the key and the secret.
  3. In Money Tree AI open Settings → Money & keys, paste them, and press Save. The app checks them with Alpaca first.
Keys are stored encrypted on this PC for your Windows user only. The screen never shows them again, only the last four characters. Without keys the app still runs on simulated money with free public prices.

4. Connect a free AI

An AI explains trades, answers questions in the chat and judges breaking news. Without it the bot still trades on its rules, but news trading only acts on clear first-order news.

  1. Make a free key at console.groq.com (or Google Gemini).
  2. Open Settings → AI, choose Use free Groq, paste the key.

If one AI is busy or out of credit, the app uses another one you connected.

5. Practice and real money

ModeWhat happens
SimulationNo keys: real prices, money exists only on this PC.
Practice (paper)Alpaca paper account: real prices, real order flow, practice money.
Real moneyYour Alpaca live account. Needs live keys, a typed confirmation, and arming.

Run on practice money first and look at the results in Home. To switch, open Modes → Money or Settings → Money & keys. The app restarts on the other account.

Money moves in and out only in Alpaca's own app or website. Money Tree AI never asks for bank details and never moves money.

6. Arm, Stop and Start

ArmWith real money the bot only watches until you press Arm real money. It disarms itself at the daily loss limit, when you press Disarm, and when the app closes. After each start of the app, arm it again.
STOPThe red button at the top right. From that moment the bot buys nothing and sells nothing on a signal. Stop-loss and take-profit stay active. Stop is remembered after a restart.
StartPress it to let the bot trade again. You can also type stop or start in the chat.

The Is it trading? card on Home tells you at once if the bot only watches and why (for example "real money is not armed"), with the button that fixes it. It also counts today's buys that did not happen, by reason.

8. The screens

  • Home: your money, whether it is trading, what it holds, today's result.
  • Live: every step the bot takes, as it happens.
  • Brain: news decisions (Reflex), company checks (Picks), the news radar, what it learned, and Memory.
  • Swarm: the strategy bots and the strategy the account trades with.
  • Research, Chart, Market, News, Catalysts, Activity: look up any stock and follow events.
  • Settings: getting started, keys, AI, language, where your data lives.
  • The AI panel on the left: ask anything, for example "why no trades?".

9. How it trades on news

  1. Read. Official sources (central banks, the White House, the SEC, company newsrooms, press wires) and Alpaca's news wire, every few seconds.
  2. Ripple. Who the news is about, and who else it affects (suppliers, customers, power, policy).
  3. Think. The AI works through the event and rates each stock: up or down, how sure, for how long.
  4. Confirm. The price must agree within minutes: a small rise, but not a move that is already over. Penny stocks and thin stocks are skipped.
  5. Act. The buy passes the same gates as every trade, with a time limit. Bad news on a stock it holds can sell it.

10. How it gets better while you use it

  • Strategy bots breed and test thousands of strategies on months of real prices. A new one replaces the current one only if it wins on prices no bot trained on. Each bot keeps its best finds between runs.
  • Learning from results: every buy signal is followed to the end. Setups that lose are made smaller or blocked.
  • News memory: every news idea is checked against the real price a trading day later. Kinds of news that worked are trusted more, kinds that did not are trusted less (at most 30% either way), and the AI sees this record before it judges new news.
  • You can see all of it in Brain → Memory, with the bot's level and how it grew.

11. Memory and your data

Keys, settings and everything the bot learned are kept in one folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BofStudios\Money Tree AI. Every new version uses this folder, so an update never starts the bot over. Closing and opening the app keeps everything too, except arming.

  • Back up: close the app (tray icon → Quit) and copy that folder.
  • New PC: copy the folder to the same place on the new PC. Enter your Alpaca and AI keys again: they are encrypted for one Windows user and cannot be read on another PC.
  • Start over: Brain → What it learned → Forget, or delete the folder while the app is closed.

12. Safety limits

LimitWhat it does
Risk per tradeThe money lost if a stop-loss fills is a fixed small part of the account (at most 1% in the sold edition).
Daily loss limitWhen the day's losses reach it (at most 3%), real money disarms for the day. A restart does not reset it.
PositionsAt most 5 stocks at once, at most 25% of the account in one stock.
Stop-loss and targetEvery buy has both. Whole-share buys keep them at Alpaca, so they work even when your PC is off.
PausesA cooldown after each trade, a pause after several stop-outs, and a pause on a large drawdown.

You can make these limits smaller in config\config.yaml in the data folder, never larger than the edition allows.

13. Phone access

On the same Wi-Fi, open the tray icon menu → Copy phone link and open it on your phone. The link holds a private password, so other people on the network cannot control the bot. Do not share the link.

14. Licence

  • At the first start, enter the licence key from your purchase e-mail.
  • To move to another PC: Settings → Licence → Deactivate, then activate on the new PC.
  • One licence is for one person. The app checks the licence online now and then.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

1. Kurulum

  1. MoneyTreeAI-Setup.exe dosyasını çalıştır. Windows 10 ya da 11, 64 bit. Yaklaşık 1 GB boş alan ve 8 GB bellek yeterli.
  2. Windows SmartScreen "Windows bilgisayarınızı korudu" derse Ek bilgi, sonra Yine de çalıştır'a tıkla. Bu, henüz çok indirilmemiş yeni programlarda çıkar.

2. İlk açılış

Uygulama birkaç soru sorar: dil, işlemleri ne kadar tutacağın, hangi piyasa ve alıma kimin karar vereceği. Her cevap botun ne yaptığını değiştirir. Hepsini sonra Modlar ve Ayarlar'dan değiştirebilirsin.

Sonra strateji botlarının bilgisayarın ne kadarını kullanacağını sorar. Tam en hızlısıdır. Hafif bilgisayarı serin tutar.

3. Alpaca bağlantısı

Bot sadece Alpaca üzerinden işlem yapar: API'si olan, ABD hisselerinde komisyon almayan bir ABD aracı kurumu. Birçok ülkeden hesap açılabilir; ülkeni kabul ettiğini kontrol et.

  1. Alpaca hesabı aç. Paper (deneme) hesabı ücretsizdir ve hemen hazırdır.
  2. Alpaca'da API Keys kısmını açıp bir anahtar çifti oluştur. Anahtarı ve gizli anahtarı kopyala.
  3. Money Tree AI'da Ayarlar → Para ve anahtarlar'ı aç, yapıştır ve Kaydet'e bas. Uygulama önce Alpaca ile kontrol eder.
Anahtarlar bu bilgisayarda, sadece senin Windows kullanıcın için şifreli saklanır. Ekran onları bir daha göstermez, sadece son dört karakteri. Anahtar olmadan uygulama ücretsiz açık fiyatlarla simüle parayla çalışır.

4. Ücretsiz yapay zekâ

Yapay zekâ işlemleri açıklar, sohbette soruları cevaplar ve son dakika haberleri değerlendirir. Olmadan da bot kurallarıyla işlem yapar, ama haberle işlem sadece açık, birinci derece haberlerde hareket eder.

  1. console.groq.com adresinden ücretsiz anahtar al (ya da Google Gemini).
  2. Ayarlar → Yapay zekâ'yı aç, Ücretsiz Groq kullan'ı seç, anahtarı yapıştır.

Bir yapay zekâ meşgulse ya da kredisi bittiyse uygulama bağladığın diğerini kullanır.

9. Haberle nasıl işlem yapar

  1. Okur. Resmî kaynaklar (merkez bankaları, Beyaz Saray, SEC, şirket haber odaları, basın ajansları) ve Alpaca'nın haber akışı, birkaç saniyede bir.
  2. Dalga. Haber kimin hakkında ve başka kimleri etkiler (tedarikçiler, müşteriler, enerji, politika).
  3. Düşünür. Yapay zekâ olayı adım adım inceler ve her hisseyi puanlar: yukarı ya da aşağı, ne kadar emin, ne kadar süre.
  4. Onaylar. Fiyat dakikalar içinde katılmalı: küçük bir yükseliş, ama bitmiş bir hareket değil. Küçük ve az işlem gören hisseler atlanır.
  5. Hareket eder. Alım, her işlemle aynı kapılardan süre sınırıyla geçer. Elindeki hisseye kötü haber gelirse satabilir.

10. Kullandıkça nasıl gelişir

  • Strateji botları aylarca gerçek fiyat üzerinde binlerce strateji üretir ve dener. Yenisi, sadece hiçbir botun eğitilmediği fiyatlarda kazanırsa mevcut olanın yerine geçer. Her bot en iyi bulduklarını çalışmalar arasında saklar.
  • Sonuçlardan öğrenme: her alım sinyali sonuna kadar izlenir. Kaybettiren kurulumlar küçültülür ya da engellenir.
  • Haber hafızası: her haber fikri bir işlem günü sonra gerçek fiyatla karşılaştırılır. İşe yarayan haber türlerine daha çok, yaramayanlara daha az güvenilir (her iki yöne en fazla %30) ve yapay zekâ yeni haberi değerlendirmeden önce bu kaydı görür.
  • Hepsini Beyin → Hafıza'da botun seviyesi ve nasıl geliştiğiyle birlikte görebilirsin.

Anahtarlar, ayarlar ve botun öğrendiği her şey tek klasörde durur: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BofStudios\Money Tree AI. Her yeni sürüm bu klasörü kullanır, yani güncelleme botu asla sıfırdan başlatmaz. Uygulamayı kapatıp açmak da kurmak dışında her şeyi korur.

  • Sıfırdan başla: Beyin → Öğrendikleri → Unut, ya da uygulama kapalıyken klasörü sil.
